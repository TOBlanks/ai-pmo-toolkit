# AI PMO-in-a-Box Toolkit

This project demonstrates how AI can accelerate **Project Management Office (PMO)** functions by embedding AI-powered prompts and automation into everyday templates.

---

## 📂 What’s Inside
- **Project Charter Template (with AI prompts):** Automatically generate scope, objectives, and success criteria.  
- **Risk Log with AI Analysis:** Uses AI prompts to suggest risk categories, mitigation strategies, and likelihood/impact scoring.  
- **Action Item Tracker:** Structured prompts for summarizing meeting notes into clear action items and assigning accountability.  

---

## 💡 Why It Matters
Traditional PMO reporting and documentation can take hours. By embedding AI into everyday templates, PMOs can:
- Reduce reporting cycle time by **40%+**  
- Improve decision-making with AI-assisted risk identification  
- Enable faster onboarding for new PMs with guided prompts  

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- **ChatGPT** (prompt design)  
- **Smartsheet AI** (workflow automation)  
- **Microsoft Excel/Word templates**
